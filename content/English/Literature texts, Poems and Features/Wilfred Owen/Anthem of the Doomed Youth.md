# Poem

What passing-bells for these who die as cattle?\ <span>  </span>—Only the monstrous anger of the guns.\ <span>  </span>Only the stuttering rifles' rapid rattle\
Can patter out their hasty orisons.\
No mockeries now for them; no prayers nor bells;  <span>  </span>Nor any voice of mourning save the choirs,—
The shrill, demented choirs of wailing shells;\ <span>  </span>And bugles calling for them from sad shires.

What candles may be held to speed them all? <span>  </span>Not in the hands of boys, but in their eyes
Shall shine the holy glimmers of goodbyes.\ <span>  </span>The pallor of girls' brows shall be their pall;\
Their flowers the tenderness of patient minds,\
And each slow dusk a drawing-down of blinds.
