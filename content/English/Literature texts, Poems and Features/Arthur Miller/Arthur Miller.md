# Who is Arthur Miller

Arthur Miller (1915-2005) was an influential American playwright, essayist, and prominent figure in 20th-century American theatre. His works often explore themes of morality, social responsibility, and the human condition, reflecting the complexities of American life. Miller's most notable plays, including "Death of a Salesman," "The Crucible," and "A View from the Bridge," have left a lasting impact on both literature and the performing arts. This document delves into his life, major works, and the legacy he left behind.
